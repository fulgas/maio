# Your first song, on air

In about fifteen minutes you make a song, put it in a playlist, play it, and put the playlist on air for anyone with the link. You need Maio running ([Quick start](../../README.md#quick-start)) and an engine that is ready: the chip icon in the header shows a green dot.

## Make a song

1. On **Songs**, describe the song in **What it sounds like**, for example *warm pads, slow, rain on the window*.
2. Pick a mood, a genre and a tempo with the chips under the box, and a length with the slider among them, or leave them as they are. Leave **Vocals** off for an instrumental.
3. Press **Make**. The song appears under **Being made**, with its progress and the time left.
4. When it is done it moves to your songs, with a title. Press its play button to hear it.

   ![Making a song, and the song when it is done](../images/manual/make-4.png)

Every Make is a new take with a new seed, so the same description makes a different song each time.

## Put it in a playlist

1. On the shelf above the songs, press the **+** tile, name the playlist, and press **Create**. Its page opens.

   ![Making a playlist from the shelf](../images/manual/playlist-1.png)

2. Go back to **Songs**, press the add-to-playlist button on your song, and pick the playlist.

   ![Adding the song to the playlist](../images/manual/playlist-2.png)

3. Open the playlist from its tile on the shelf and press the play button on its band. The player bar at the bottom plays it.

## Put it on air

1. On the playlist's page, turn on the **Live** switch in the strip under the band. The playlist now plays on the server, for everyone at the same moment.

   ![Turning on Live](../images/manual/live-1.png)

2. Copy the MP3 address with its copy button and open it in another browser tab, or in a player such as VLC. You hear what every listener hears.

   ![Copying the MP3 address](../images/manual/live-2.png)

3. Make the playlist **private** in the strip to give it a secret link instead: only people with the link can listen, and a new link stops the old one.
4. Turn **Live** off to take it off air.
